# Phase 15 live: the reading before the brief

Measured on 23 November 2026, on the owner's go-ahead, on gpt-4.1 on Copilot, at commit `9787cf2` (phase 15 merged)
and then at `9305363` (the fix this run called for). One throwaway repository made for it: a small Python client of
an orders API, `src/demo/client.py` with two functions calling httpx, one test, and httpx 0.28.1 installed in its
`.venv` (seven distributions in all); `adk-spec init --no-explore`, so no profile. The request, the same both times:

> Give every HTTP call in src/demo/client.py a timeout of ten seconds, and retry a call once when it times out.

## Run 1, at `9787cf2`: failed at the deltas

8 calls (triage 1, scout 1, survey 1, review 1, brief 1, deltas 3), 13,243 tokens, 29 s. Nothing written.

- **The order held.** Triage sized it `lean`, touching `http-client`; the reading ran next, aimed by the request,
  and kept 5 statements, naming 4 unknowns; the brief came after it, and stated the unknowns as assumptions ("retry
  attempts are only to be made in the event of a timeout"), as a lean brief asks nothing.
- **The brief split the change.** Told nothing of what triage found, it named three capabilities, `http-request`,
  `resilience` and `timeout`, for two functions in one file. The deltas stage, bound to one delta a capability,
  left one without a purpose and another with no requirement in each of its three tries.

## Run 2, at `9305363`: the change written

The fix: the brief is given the capabilities triage found, and told to name as few as hold the change, each with a
requirement at least.

9 calls (triage 1, scout 1, survey 1, review 1, brief 1, deltas 2, tasks 1), 13,394 tokens, 24 s. A lean change:
brief, one delta (`http-client`, one requirement, three scenarios), `context.md` with 5 statements and 2 unknowns,
four tasks.

**What was right.** One capability, triage's. Every statement's quote is in its file, and they name where the change
plugs in (`httpx.get` in `fetch_user`, the `httpx.Client` in `fetch_orders`) and how tests are written here
(monkeypatched `httpx.get`). The tasks put three tests first, at the functions a caller uses, then the code.

**What was not.**

- One scenario's THEN, "after a brief delay, the user data is returned", is no observation a run could show false,
  and its GIVEN says the server is unreachable where the requirement speaks of a timeout. A lean change owes no
  check, which would have named it.
- The scout read the project's files and `pyproject.toml`, and no installed library: httpx's own default timeout of
  five seconds, the fact the change most depends on, was not looked up. The libraries were listed to it.
- The question rounds were not exercised: a lean change asks none.

## Run 3, at `726f7e4`: a standard change, and its check

The owner's go-ahead for one more run, after the scout was told that a change setting or relying on a library's
option or default depends on that library. The request, on the standard tier, answers piped in (`yes`, then `=`):

> Set the orders client's HTTP timeout to ten seconds instead of the library's default, and retry a call once when
> it times out.

`propose`: 6 calls (scout 1, survey 1, review 1, brief 1, deltas 1, tasks 1), 9,631 tokens, 15 s. `approve`: the
check, 1 call, 1,393 tokens, 0 findings; approved.

- **No library was read, again.** The scout was shown the libraries, httpx 0.28.1 among them, and the new rule, and
  named the project's three files and four searches. The review then named the very unknown, "How is httpx timeout
  currently set ...? Where: httpx documentation", and could not ask for it: the survey and the review are never
  shown the libraries, only the scout is. A gap in the design: the stages that find what is missing cannot name it.
- **No question was asked on a standard change.** The brief left no open point, and settled by assumption that the
  change is to `fetch_orders` alone, where "the orders client" may mean the module, `fetch_user` too. The check
  found nothing: the requirement and the brief agree with each other.
- **The scenarios are observable** this time ("raises or propagates a timeout error observable by the caller").

## Run 4, at `013da92`: the survey asks for httpx, by the wrong name

The owner's go-ahead for one more run, after the survey and the review were shown the libraries too. The same
standard request. `propose`: 8 calls (scout 1, survey 3, review 1, brief 1, deltas 1, tasks 1), 13,744 tokens, 289
s. Written: a standard draft, one capability, `context.md` with 5 statements (2 left out, their quotes not in their
files) and 2 unknowns.

- **A stage reached for a library**, the first time: the survey asked for httpx's own `_client.py`. It named it
  `python:.venv/lib/python3.11/site-packages/httpx/_client.py`, the environment's path on disk with a Python version
  of its own making, and was told there was no such file: `python:<path>` had been read as a path on disk.
- **The brief asked nothing again** on a standard change, and settled what it could not know as assumptions.

Fixed after it: a library's path named through its environment's folder is read by its name inside the library,
looked up in the list as before; and the list of libraries shows a real path of each environment's to name a file
by, `python:httpx/__init__.py`.

## What this changes

Run 2's fix, merged. After run 3: the survey and the review to be shown the installed libraries too, so that the
stage that names an unknown in a library can ask to read it; and a brief that settles a real ambiguity by assumption
on a standard change is still to be looked at.
